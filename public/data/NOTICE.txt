The catalog bundled here is a filtered derivative: all 110 Messier objects,
plus NGC/IC objects with V (or B) magnitude 13.0 or brighter, plus objects that
have no magnitude but a major axis of at least 2 arcminutes. Stars, duplicates,
and nonexistent entries are omitted. M102 is identified as NGC 5866, the
Spindle Galaxy. Changes from the original database are that filter and that
identification. Share-alike applies to catalog.json.

Altitude, azimuth, sidereal time, precession, and refraction use
astronomy-engine by Don Cross, MIT license.
https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy

The Messier captured seed, when present, is derived from Vardan Antonyan's
private spreadsheet and is not part of the OpenNGC license.
